About me.

My favourite part of being a designer is seeing my work out in the real world.

Hi, I'm Delia, a graphic designer specialising in branding, FMCG packaging, print and digital design.

Whether it's packaging on a supermarket shelf, an advert in print or something I've designed being used at an event, there's something really satisfying about seeing an idea become a finished piece of work.

I'm naturally a blue-sky thinker. I love coming up with lots of ideas, exploring different directions and working through the process of finding what works best. I enjoy collaborating with others, sharing ideas and getting feedback along the way. I'm also comfortable working independently and taking a project from the initial idea through to the final design.

I like being involved in the whole creative process, from the early brainstorming stages to refining the smaller details. Seeing how a design develops along the way is one of the things I enjoy most about what I do.

A little outside of design.

Outside of design, I love spending time with my family, animals, being out in nature and playing video games. I also run a small beauty-focused social media account, which gives me another creative outlet and helps me keep up with what's happening online. I find inspiration in all sorts of places, from nature and everyday experiences to the things I come across on social media.
